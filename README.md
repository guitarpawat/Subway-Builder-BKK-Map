# Subway Builder BKK Map

Difficulty: Easy

Connect Bangkok’s bustling neighborhoods, cross the Chao Phraya, and bring the
city closer by rail.

Build across Greater Bangkok, from the Gulf of Thailand north to Bang Pa-in.
The map includes English labels and estimated travel demand for commuters,
airports, universities, hospitals, schools, parks and leisure destinations.
Around **15 million residents** are represented across the playable area.

## Download and play

1. Download **BKK.zip** from this repository’s **Releases** page.
2. Open Railyard and choose **Library → Import → Choose ZIP(s)**.
3. Select **BKK.zip**, then launch Subway Builder through Railyard.
4. Choose **Bangkok** and start a new game.

## About the data

Population is based on the 2025 Thai census, with estimated neighborhood distribution.
Workplace demand uses official factory, Social Security and district employment
evidence. Travel patterns remain estimates, not an official transport forecast.
Some destinations and water depths use documented assumptions.
